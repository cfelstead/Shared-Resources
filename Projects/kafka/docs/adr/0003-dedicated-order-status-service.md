# Give OrderStatus its own service rather than folding it into NotificationService

NotificationService already fans in from three topics (`payment-failed`, `inventory-backorder`, `order-shipped`), so it was tempting to extend it to also serve OrderStatus. We chose a separate **OrderStatusService** instead, consuming all 6 topics and exposing `GET /orders/{id}` plus a SignalR hub for the UI. NotificationService's responsibility is narrow and customer-facing (send a message at specific failure/completion points); OrderStatusService's responsibility is broad and query-facing (track every Order's position across the whole pipeline). Merging them would make one service do two unrelated jobs and would require NotificationService to consume topics it has no notification behavior for. Splitting them also gives the demo a second, differently-shaped fan-in consumer to show alongside NotificationService's.
